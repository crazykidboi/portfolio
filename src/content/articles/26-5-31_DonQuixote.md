# Don Quixote with West Point Ballet

I've worked with this group before, but this felt like an even higher level production. Though there may not have been a lot of light cues to be made, a lot of this show was making sure all the lights were focused properly. I spent a lot of time fine tuning focus to make the show the best it could be, I even fixed a few lights and pitched some patch information.

As someone who has not done sound mixing full time for a few years its nice to be able to have a chance to stretch that muscle.
